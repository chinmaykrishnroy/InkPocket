# InkPocket sample

This is a **Markdown** document stored on the reader.

## Controls

- Click to scroll down.
- Double click to scroll up.
- Triple click to start or stop auto-scroll.
- Hold to return to the library.

## A short note

The display is a 200 by 200 monochrome e-paper panel. Markdown is rendered
locally from LittleFS, so it remains readable without Wi-Fi.

> Upload your own `.md` files through the Wi-Fi web page.
